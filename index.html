Curiosity below the abstraction.

LowLevel Labs is a technical knowledge space for embedded software, Linux internals, firmware, processor architecture, and hands-on debugging. The goal is simple: explain how systems work beneath the APIs—and share practical notes that help engineers reason from hardware to software.
